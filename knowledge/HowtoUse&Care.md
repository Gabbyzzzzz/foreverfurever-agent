# **How to Use & Care – TravelStar Companion (Urn)**

## **Setting the Memory**

### **Opening the Urn**

Gently rotate the base clockwise or counter-clockwise to open.

For added security, we recommend placing the ashes in a sealed velvet or poly-bag before inserting them inside the urn.

---

## **Handling with Care**

### **Cleaning**

Gently dust with a soft, dry microfiber cloth.

Do not use water or chemical cleaners, as they may affect the texture and finish.

---

### **Environment**

To preserve color and material quality:

- Keep indoors

- Avoid direct sunlight

- Avoid high heat sources such as radiators

---

# **How to Use & Care – Eternal Glow (Night Lamp)**

## **Setting Up the Lightbox**

Connect the lamp using the provided USB cable or battery option (depending on your model).

For the best glowing effect, place the lightbox against a neutral-colored wall so the 3D details can stand out clearly.

---

## **Handling with Care**

### **Cleaning**

Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth to gently remove dust.

Avoid water or chemical cleaners to protect the surface and internal lighting components.

---

### **Environment**

For best longevity:

- Use indoors only

- Keep away from moisture

- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight or high heat
