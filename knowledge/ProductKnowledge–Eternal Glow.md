**Eternal Glow – Product Overview & Customization**

**Product Concept**

**Eternal Glow – A Soulful Tribute, Sculpted in Light**

Eternal Glow is a personalized nightlight that transforms your pet’s photo into a three-dimensional illuminated relief.

Each piece is carefully sculpted using high-precision composite materials to create depth, texture, and a soft glowing effect that brings your pet’s memory to life.

It is designed as both a heartfelt memorial and an elegant home décor piece, providing warmth and comfort in any room.
