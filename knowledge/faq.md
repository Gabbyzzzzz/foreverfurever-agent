## **Shipping & Delivery**

### **Q: When will I receive my order?**

Each piece is custom made with care:

- Artisan Production: 6–7 business days

- Express Shipping: 3–5 business days

Total estimated time: **10–13 business days**

If you order multiple items, they will ship together once all personalized pieces are completed.

---

### **Q: Do I have to pay customs duties or taxes?**

No. We provide **Delivered Duty Paid (DDP)** service worldwide.

All taxes and customs fees are included at checkout.

There are no hidden charges upon delivery.

---

### **Q: What if my package is delayed or lost?**

If tracking shows no movement for 10 days, please contact us and we will investigate.

If the package is confirmed lost, we will resend your order free of charge.

If it shows “Delivered” but you cannot find it, please check with neighbors first.

## **Security & Care**

### **Q: Is the TravelStar urn safe for air travel?**

Yes. It is designed for travel.

Each companion includes:

- An airtight sealing liner bag

- A secure magnetic closure

This dual-layer protection keeps remains safe during transport.

The stone-like material is lightweight (about 210g), making it easy to carry.

---

### **Q: How secure are the ashes during travel?**

Each TravelStar Companion includes a precision-fit sealing liner bag.

Combined with the magnetic closure, this double-layer protection ensures the remains stay secure and undisturbed throughout your journey.

---

### **Q: How do I care for my memorial?**

Simply wipe the surface with the included microfiber cloth.

Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners to preserve the artistic finish.
